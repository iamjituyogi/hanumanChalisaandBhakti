Privacy Policy

Hanuman Chalisa & Bhakti (हनुमान चालीसा एवं भक्ति) · Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Hanuman Chalisa & Bhakti ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app offers Hanuman Chalisa, Bajrang Baan, Hanuman Ashtak, Aarti and devotional bhajans as audio and text, along with a Jaap (chanting) counter. It is developed by Jitendra Yogi.

In short: the app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require sign-up or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files or location.
  • We do not show advertisements and do not use advertising IDs.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.

3. Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your phone and never sent to us:

  • Your preferences: theme (light/dark/system), playback speed, "resume where I left off" and "auto-play next" settings, and text size.
  • Your favourite bhajans and your listening history / continue-listening position.
  • Your Jaap counter totals and daily chanting record.

You can clear your listening history from Settings, or remove all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

4. Internet use and Firebase

Reading the texts and playing the audio does not need an internet connection. The app uses the internet only to check whether a newer version of the app is required. For this it reads a small configuration value (the required app version and update link) from Google Firebase Realtime Database.

No personal information from you is written to this database. As with any online service, Google Firebase may automatically process technical data such as your IP address and app installation identifiers to deliver the service. Google handles this under its own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet – to check for a required app update.
  • Notifications – to show playback controls while audio plays in the background.
  • Foreground service (media playback) – so audio keeps playing when the screen is off or you switch apps.
  • Vibrate – haptic feedback, for example when counting Jaap.
  • Set wallpaper – used only when you choose to set a deity image as your wallpaper. The app does not read your existing wallpaper.

6. Sharing

If you use a share button, the content you choose (such as a shlok or a link) is passed to the app you select, such as WhatsApp or Messages. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

7. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Data security and retention

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. We keep no copy of it, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our servers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date changed accordingly.
